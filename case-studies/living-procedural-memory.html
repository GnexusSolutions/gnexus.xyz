The operating problem

What needed to become legible.

Long projects often decay through a coherent hallucination: each new instruction becomes a fresh interpretation, valid work is silently replaced, and the team loses the original outcome, exact source, and last aligned branch point.

Every new message should be a delta—not a reset.

The intervention

A bounded operating method.

  • Preserve original intent, artifact custody, and the current real state.
  • Treat every new instruction as a delta against that state.
  • Locate the earliest divergence rather than rewriting the whole project.
  • Classify material as KEEP, REPAIR, REHOME, QUARANTINE, DEFER, or REJECT.
  • Repair one difficult end-to-end canary and prove the actual runtime before expanding.
