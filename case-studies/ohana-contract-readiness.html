The operating problem

What needed to become legible.

Business documents can stall because no one has a shared view of what exists, which version is current, what information is missing, who is authorized to review, what is ready for signature, and what must happen after execution.

Control document state without pretending to practice law.

The intervention

A bounded operating method.

  • Create a document register with owner, state, version, and next action.
  • Separate draft, review, blocked, signature-ready, executed, and follow-through states.
  • Route review only through authorized people and preserve the decision boundary.
  • Track missing information, blockers, dependencies, and deadlines.
  • Issue a completion or follow-through record rather than assuming signature ends the process.
