OhanaVista Contract Readiness

Administrative document operations that preserve custody, status, review authority, and follow-through without crossing into legal practice.

Contract operations · Document control · Executive operationsEvidence-led case study
ContextOperating problemOperating modelEvidence ledgerTruth boundaryRole relevance

Context

What made this an operating problem.

Business documents become operationally risky when drafts, review, signatures, blockers, and follow-up are tracked informally. At OhanaVista, the responsibility was to make readiness visible and actionable while keeping legal authority boundaries explicit.

Operating problem

What had to remain true.

  • Document states and owners were not always visible at a glance.
  • Review and signature could be confused with approval authority.
  • Missing information and follow-up needed accountable next actions.
  • Administrative support had to remain distinct from legal advice.

Operating model

The structure introduced.

01IntakeCapture document, purpose, parties, and missing inputs.
02CustodyMaintain version and owner visibility.
03RouteSend through authorized review pathways.
04ReadyConfirm signature prerequisites and blockers.
05FollowTrack execution, storage, and next actions.

Evidence ledger

Direct work

States

Draft, review, blocked, ready, executed, follow-up.

Workflow

Custody, routing, signatures, and follow-through.

Confidential documents are not published.

Transfer

Applicable to legal operations and client intake roles.

No law license or paralegal credential is claimed.

Truth boundary

A portfolio is only useful when its limits are visible.

Boundary

This case supports contract-readiness, document-control, and administrative coordination capability. It explicitly excludes legal advice, representation, legal conclusions, and unauthorized document disclosure.

Role relevance

Where this proof transfers.

Contract administrator, legal operations coordinator, legal project coordinator, intake coordinator, executive operations.
