The operating problem

What needed to become legible.

A process can be technically correct and still fail the person using it. Requirements are scattered, decisions are invisible, language is inaccessible, handoffs break, and the next action is unclear—especially under stress.

Adoption begins with what a person can understand and do next.

The intervention

A bounded operating method.

  • Start with the person’s actual objective, current capacity, and immediate barrier.
  • Translate complex requirements into plain next actions without erasing important conditions.
  • Track missing information, owners, handoffs, and recourse.
  • Use feedback and repeated friction to improve the process.
  • Keep dignity, consent, and truth boundaries inside the operating design.
