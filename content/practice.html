01 — The practice

The work is not the tool.
The work is the connection.

My craft is the disciplined movement from an unclear situation to a shared, executable, and inspectable operating state.

  1. 01

    Orient

    Establish the real objective, present conditions, decision authority, risk, and next action before adding a system.

  2. 02

    Structure

    Connect intake, owners, decisions, states, artifacts, dependencies, blockers, and handoffs.

  3. 03

    Translate

    Move between user language, operational reality, executive context, and technical requirements without flattening any of them.

  4. 04

    Verify

    Separate what exists from what is planned through tests, evidence, receipts, failure states, and explicit boundaries.

“A useful system does not merely produce an answer. It preserves enough reality that a person can understand what happened, act with authority, and recover when the path changes.”
