## About

I make the work hold together.

My career has crossed store and restaurant management, wireless customer education, housing navigation, security, commercial installation, contract readiness, business legal-document administration, AI workflow development, and executive technology leadership. The environments changed, but the responsibility repeated: understand what someone is trying to accomplish, make the process legible, coordinate the people and information involved, resolve blockers, and verify that the intended outcome was actually reached.

As Chief Technology & Innovation Officer at OhanaVista, I lead operational support and systems development that converts business needs into documented workflows, responsibilities, decision points, and usable operating processes. I also coordinate contract readiness and business legal-document administration through organized status tracking, authorized review, signature readiness, and follow-through.

Separately, through Gnexus Consulting LLC, I design AI-enabled workflows, operating specifications, testing criteria, risk boundaries, evidence rules, and operational handoffs. My interest is not AI theater. It is whether a system remains oriented, respects human authority, exposes its actual state, survives failure, and produces evidence another person can use.

Earlier in my career, I managed stores and trained managers, educated wireless customers, resolved billing complaints and escalations, helped 32 shelter clients navigate barriers toward housing, monitored high-accountability sites, and coordinated commercial installation work among crews, customers, vendors, and active site conditions.

I am pursuing work across customer success, implementation, business operations, AI operations, customer enablement, contract and document operations, community programs, and field service delivery.

I do not present transferable experience as formal enterprise SaaS tenure, administrative legal-document support as legal practice, or locally verified systems as public production. I prefer an honest capability boundary and a clear plan to earn the remaining evidence.
