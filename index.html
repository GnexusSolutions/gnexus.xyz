02

Position in reality

My career is not a detour from systems work. It is the source of my systems judgment.

I learned systems from the places where a missing handoff becomes a person waiting, a crew blocked on site, a customer unable to act, a document stalled between states, or an AI confidently describing work that never happened.

That proximity changed what I optimize for. Not appearance of control. Orientation, authority, progress, evidence, and recourse.

My place is close enough to consequence to notice what abstraction erased—and technical enough to rebuild the connection.
